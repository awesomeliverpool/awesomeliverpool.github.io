Between 2014 and 2025 we gave away over £65,000 in microgrants…

As one of the UK’s most active chapters of the international Awesome Foundation, our application form was famously simple, and all of our grants were no strings attached and reporting-free.

…But sadly, we closed in 2026

After our last Dean stepped down in December 2025, we took the difficult decision to put Awesome Liverpool into hibernation until a new group of trustees could come along.

Interested in restarting it?

What’s the history of Awesome Liverpool?

We were the Liverpool chapter of the international Awesome Foundation.

In 2014, a group of friends in the Liverpool tech sector realised there wasn’t a functioning Awesome chapter in the UK yet, so they set one up. For over a decade after that, we were joined by a stunning roster of volunteer trustees, from all sorts of backgrounds, each donating their time, expertise, and usually £50 a month, to help make awesome projects happen in and around Liverpool.

Anyone in the Liverpool City Region could apply for a £500 gift, and we particularly welcomed applications from people who wouldn’t normally apply to the big funding bodies. There was no limit to the number of times you could apply (or the number of times you could win) and we judged each application on its own merits.

We generally liked to see projects that:

  1. Solved a problem
  2. Had a clear budget
  3. Benefitted Liverpool
  4. Brought joy to the world

Our £500 gifts were no strings attached – there was no reporting or checking up. We trusted our winners to spend the £500 wisely, furthering Awesome in the universe.

And boy did they do just that. You can see all of our winners on the Awesome Foundation website.

Want to make Liverpool awesome again?

While Awesome Liverpool has been in hiberation since December 2025, we’d love a new group of trustees to come along and restart it.

We have a model constitution you could re-use (technically we were only ever an ‘unincorporated association’ and our ‘trustees’ weren’t trustees in any formal sense), as well as a Gmail account, social media profiles, and GoCardless account you could use.

All you’d need to do is bring a couple of like-minded friends, each happy to donate a set amount (we chose £50) each month, and a load of enthusiasm to recruit a full roster of 10 trustees, and start promoting the chapter again.

If you’re interested, email liverpool@awesomefoundation.org.
